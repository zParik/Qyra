DejaVu Sans is the default layout/render font for the Word engine.

Place DejaVuSans.ttf next to this file (src-tauri/assets/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf).
It is required at build time (included via include_bytes! in
src-tauri/src/layout/font.rs).

DejaVu fonts are based on Bitstream Vera with a permissive, redistribution-
friendly license. Source: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/

Full license text: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
